Roadmap status is a documented baseline, last reviewed 27 October 2026. Only the paper snapshot is read dynamically. Refresh this page for new published counts; manual gates do not auto-complete.

  1. 1 · NEXT · Suggested planning window: 27–30 Oct

    Repair and freeze the research protocol

    • Resolve the listed 162 combinations versus the ≤54 cap; specify the exact subset, opposite-channel exit and selection rule.
    • Reserve genuinely unseen timestamps. Record file hashes, TRAIN/OOS boundaries and all exclusions before looking at results.
    • Do not re-tune rejected TP, ATR, session or RSI candidates on inspected history.

    Exit gate: reviewed, versioned pre-registration. If no unseen data exists, wait for collection; dates slide.

    Source: docs/research_plan_breakout_2026-10-24.md; docs/existing_gold_audit_2026-10-26.md

  2. 2 · BLOCKED BY 1 · Allow 1–2 weeks after data is available

    Test the hypothesis, then accept or reject

    • Train-select once; freeze before cold OOS. BREAKOUT-B requires mean R ≥0.12, PF ≥1.25, bootstrap 95% CI above zero (or P(net>0) ≥0.90), ≥3/4 profitable folds, ≥1.5 trades/day and OOS drawdown ≤1.3× baseline.
    • Research acceptance is not LIVE approval. The live-readiness review separately requires ≥100 genuinely cold-OOS trades, mean-R CI above zero and 4/4 profitable walk-forward quarters.
    • If the hypothesis fails, close it. Start a new pre-registration, not another optimized re-split.

    Exit gate: dated result report and explicit decision. No profitable candidate is guaranteed.

  3. 3 · PARALLEL PREPARATION · Allow 1–2 weeks; evidence still pending

    Prove the execution and account safeguards

    • Run the 20-trade demo drill at 0.01 lots: requested vs filled price, attached SL, BE modify round-trip, stop-distance and filling-mode acceptance, and pre-agreed realized-R tolerance.
    • Match paper/backtest/LIVE entry timing, bid/ask costs, BE, weekend exits and intrabar assumptions. Existing M1 ends before this paper book and cannot validate it.
    • Drill kill switch and latched equity floor. Verify monitoring, restart/reconnect behaviour, dashboard access protection and broker minimum volume.
    • Use a separate Gold account or implement a hard combined account-risk gate. Agree affordable funding and loss limits; $300 is not evidence of adequate live capital.

    Exit gate: demo execution report plus account-risk sign-off. Stop/BE code exists, but fake-terminal tests are not a broker drill.

    Sources: docs/live_readiness_review_2026-10-23.md §6, docs/live_protection_2026-10-23.md, STATUS.md

  4. 4 · AFTER A VALIDATED STRATEGY IS FROZEN · Weeks to months

    Collect qualifying forward evidence

    • Run the exact strategy and exit engine intended for LIVE. A material strategy change starts a new evidence cohort; do not carry over unrelated trades.
    • Review weekly: net R, PF, bootstrap uncertainty, drawdown, costs, side imbalance and operational incidents. Do not fit BUY/SELL filters from this small sample.
    • Require ~300+ qualifying paper trades or positive mean R with bootstrap CI excluding zero. Quantity alone cannot rescue a negative or uncertain edge.

    Timing guide: from zero, 300 trades at an illustrative 1.5–3 trades per trading day takes roughly 100–200 trading days (20–40 weeks). This is not a measured forecast; unseen-data collection may add time.

  5. 5 · BLOCKED BY ALL PRIOR GATES · No target launch date

    Formal go / no-go review, then a limited pilot

    • Review research, cold OOS, forward evidence, demo execution, account controls and available capital together. Record explicit human approval; never auto-promote.
    • Only if approved: minimum-size live pilot consistent with broker limits and the signed risk budget. Keep monitoring and halt rules active.
    • Schedule a 30-day post-launch review. Stop on safety failures or breached risk limits; do not increase size to recover losses.

    Today: remain in FORWARD_TEST. The dashboard is read-only and cannot change trading mode.
